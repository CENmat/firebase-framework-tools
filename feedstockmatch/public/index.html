Why This Platform Doesn't Exist Yet

The Problem

  • Organic byproducts are generated at predictable volumes, but suppliers and processors find each other through brokers, spreadsheets, and word-of-mouth.
  • No platform integrates geospatial matching + transparent LCA estimates + compliance audit trails in one workflow.
  • Existing LCA databases (ecoinvent, GREET) are expert-only tools, not accessible to the operators who actually move feedstock.

What FeedstockMatch Solves

  • A food processor with 200 t/month of spent grain can see which AD facilities within 150 km have capacity, get a GHG estimate, verify contamination compatibility, and generate an audit record — in one session.
  • Every emission factor, every assumption, every override is logged and transparent.
  • The platform bridges three siloed systems: waste brokerage, LCA databases, and permit tracking.
